About Me

Hello! I'm Dr. Elena Papadopoulou. My passion for the ancient world was sparked by the epic tales of Homer and the profound questions posed by Plato. My academic journey has been a fascinating exploration of the language, literature, and philosophy that formed the bedrock of Western civilization. Here at Starlight University, I have the privilege of guiding students through the intricate beauty of Ancient Greek. My goal is to show that these ancient texts are not dusty relics, but living conversations that still speak to us today. From translating epic poetry to debating philosophy, I aim to make the classical world accessible, engaging, and relevant for everyone.

My Teaching Philosophy

I believe that learning Ancient Greek is like learning to decode the DNA of our own language and ideas. My classroom is a collaborative workshop where we don't just memorize grammar, but actively engage with the texts as historians, linguists, and philosophers. We work together to unravel the complexities of the language, celebrating the 'aha!' moments when a difficult passage finally clicks into place. My aim is to equip students with the skills to read these texts with confidence and to appreciate the timeless wisdom they contain.
