About Me

Bonjour! I am Dr. Isabelle Dubois. Growing up in Lyon, the culinary heart of France, I was surrounded by a rich tapestry of history, art, and of course, language. My passion is to share not just the words of the French language, but its soul—the elegance in its grammar, the romance in its poetry, and the vibrancy of its culture. I bring the atmosphere of a Parisian café into our lessons, making learning an immersive and delightful experience.

My Teaching Philosophy

I believe learning French is like learning a piece of music. It requires understanding the structure (grammar), but also feeling the rhythm and melody (pronunciation and intonation). My classroom is a collaborative space where we celebrate every success, from correctly using the subjunctive to debating a film by Jean-Luc Godard. I focus on building confidence through practical application, ensuring my students can not only pass exams but also order a croissant with perfect flair.
